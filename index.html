Provincial government digital attention and corporate financial constraints in VIETNAM: the role of digitalization and ESG

Overview: This independent research explores the influence of provincial government digital attention (PGDA) on corporate financial constraints, highlighting the mediating role of digitalization and the moderating role of ESG within the context of Vietnam's evolving digital landscape.

📰 Recognition & Publication

The findings of this research were officially published in:

"Ảnh hưởng của chuyển đổi số đến hạn chế tài chính trong các công ty niêm yết tại Việt Nam" (The Impact of Digital Transformation on Financial Constraints in Listed Companies in Vietnam) - Tạp chí Tài chính Doanh nghiệp (Corporate Finance Magazine), March 2025 Issue.

📖 View Journal Publication

🏆 This project was also awarded the 3rd Prize in the University-level Scientific Research Competition (2024-2025).

Analytical Insights & BA Mindset:

  • Data Interpretation & Root-Cause Analysis: Addressed the counter-intuitive statistical finding that Provincial Government Digital Attention (PGDA) actually increases short-term corporate financial constraints. Conducted root-cause analysis to identify that top-down digital mandates force enterprises into rapid, capital-intensive transitions, draining their immediate liquidity.
  • Strategic Problem-Solving: Translated complex econometric results into a practical business problem, demonstrating how external policy compliance creates internal financial bottlenecks and "sunk cost" burdens for companies.
  • Actionable Recommendations: Synthesized data to prove that integrating robust ESG practices acts as a crucial moderator, allowing businesses to unlock alternative financing channels and alleviate the financial stress caused by forced digitalization.

Methodology & Execution:

  • Data Collection (Python): Programmed scripts to perform web scraping across 47 provincial portals and annual reports of 400 listed companies to quantify digital attention keywords.
  • Econometric Analysis (Stata): Utilized advanced models (System-GMM, 2SLS) to ensure robustness in identifying causation, mediation, and moderation effects.
